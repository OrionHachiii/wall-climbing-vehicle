# Safety Notice

This repository documents an educational prototype. It has not been certified for consumer, industrial, or unattended use.

## Main hazards

- The exposed propeller can cause serious injury. Use a rigid guard, keep hands and loose items away, and disconnect power before adjustment.
- Lithium-polymer batteries can ignite if punctured, shorted, overcharged, over-discharged, or used outside their rated current. Use a balance charger and a fire-resistant charging location.
- Secure the vehicle during bench testing. Unexpected thrust or wheel movement can launch parts or pull cables into the propeller.
- Wear eye protection during fabrication and testing.
- Use appropriate ventilation and supervision for laser cutting, soldering, and 3D printing.
- Inspect fasteners, wiring, propeller condition, and battery temperature before every run.

Reproduction and operation are at the user's own risk. Stop immediately if the battery swells, wiring heats, the propeller is damaged, or the vehicle becomes unstable.
